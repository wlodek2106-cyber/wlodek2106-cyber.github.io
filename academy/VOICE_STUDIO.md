# Voice Studio
Browser speech synthesis preview restricted to voices reported as localService=true, no automatic fallback to remote voices, speed/pitch controls, TXT narration export, timed dubbing plan and overlap validation. Browser/OS controls actual speech engine; offline behavior and language support depend on installed voices and device. No actual audio recording/download from browser speech synthesis. Local macOS say command and optional FFmpeg conversion are manual; choose an installed offline voice. No voice cloning, automatic dubbing, paid API or media upload. Plans are transient; export before closing. CI mocks voice engine and checks controls and TXT export; real playback requires device testing. Use authorized scripts and voices.
