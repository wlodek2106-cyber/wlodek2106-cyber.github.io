# Storyboard Studio 1.0
Private text-based film projects with a shared character bible, story, style and aspect ratio. Up to 50 films per authenticated account, 60 scenes per film, 1–120 seconds of planned duration per scene. Ordering by position then ID, cumulative timeline, scene camera, action, audio and continuity notes, copy prompt and TXT export. Four interface languages. Plans are not generated video or rendered storyboards. Adapt clip length to local model limits, use authorized voices/assets and verify rights. No model execution, external media upload, paid APIs or new infrastructure. Included in Privacy Center export.
