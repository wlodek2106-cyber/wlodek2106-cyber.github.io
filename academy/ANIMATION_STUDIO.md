# Video & Animation Studio
Local image-to-motion slideshow: 1–6 user images, max 5 MB each, 2/3/5 seconds per scene, three aspect ratios, zoom/pan/still, one caption per scene. Canvas preview and silent real-time MediaRecorder video export where supported; browser chooses WebM or MP4. Stops on hidden tab. No external requests, AI inference, audio, or cloud storage. Images decoded locally and resized preserving aspect ratio. Very large decoded images may exceed device memory. No guarantee of device/codec support; CI tests geometry and routes, not real media encoding. Use authorized images. No paid API.
