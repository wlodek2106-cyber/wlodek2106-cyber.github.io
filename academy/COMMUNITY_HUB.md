# Community Hub
Authenticated student-only topics and replies, four categories, alias, consent, owner pre-moderation, withdrawal and hide. Limits: 10 posts/day and 300/account, 30/page. Public display of account IDs, private profile, email or lesson work is not automatic. No private messaging, payments, attachments or notifications. Replies to withdrawn/hidden threads are unavailable in feed. Personal data export includes own posts. Moderation uses existing owner access; reports and operational moderation policy still need review before launch. Four UI languages. Integration tests added, not yet confirmed live.
